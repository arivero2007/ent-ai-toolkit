CASE quiz: how should AI enter this task?

      
Six resident tasks. Sort each one, then read why.

    

    

      
Conserve
AI could do the thinking we are training. Resident commits first; AI comparison afterward.

      
Augment
AI only widens the case set, after the resident's own read.

      
Surrender
The task builds no competency we are responsible for. AI drafts; resident verifies.

      
Evaluate
Runs through all three: appraise the output, name failure modes, calibrate reliance.
